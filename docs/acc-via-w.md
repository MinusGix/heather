# `Acc` via `W`: the indexed-container reduction, by hand

Scheme for [[stress-proof-suite]] item 3 / [[inductive-compiler]] tier 2,
written before the code (`crates/kernel/tests/acc.rs`).

**What this round is for.** `elim_W`'s β rule synthesizes the induction
hypothesis *under a λ* — `elim_W(sup(a; f)) ⇝ s[a, f, λy. elim_W(… ; f y)]`
— and it is the only β rule in the kernel with that shape. Every other
recursor hands the step a value. `Wf`'s β has the same shape but its ih is
*transient* (`Fin`-indexed, applied at literal indices), so it does not
exercise the abstract-position case. Nothing in the frontend reaches `W`
today and nothing will before [[end-to-end-compile-instance]]
(`simulate` is fuel-indexed), so this is **insurance on trusted code**, not
frontend progress: the machinery is checked by the one thing that genuinely
needs it, `Acc`.

**What it is not.** Not a surface feature. `Acc.intro`'s field is a
function, `reject_function_fields` keeps a `Π` out of an inductive field, and
tier 2 has no generator. Everything here is hand-built kernel terms.

## 1. The problem: `Acc` is indexed, `W` is not

```text
Acc(a)  generated by   intro : (Π(b : A). R(b, a) → Acc(b)) → Acc(a)
```

`W(x : S). T` has one type, not a family. The standard reduction (indexed
containers) fords the index: build the *unindexed* tree type, define the
index of a tree, and cut down to the trees whose indices cohere.

Fix a carrier `A : Ty 0` and a relation `R : A → A → Ty 0` (both given as
codes so they can appear under `El`; §3 pins the instance).

```text
Below(a) := Σ(b : A). R(b, a)                    the positions under a
W₀       := W^{0,0}(x : A). Below(x)             the unindexed trees
```

A `W₀` node `sup(a; f)` carries its intended index `a` as its **label**, and
`f` maps each position `y : Below(a)` — a pair `(b, r)` with `r : R(b, a)` —
to a subtree. Nothing in `W₀` forces that subtree to be *about* `b`. Two
definitions close the gap:

```text
root : W₀ → A                  root(sup(a; f)) ⇝ a
                               elim_W at the constant motive A, step = a

coh  : W₀ → Ty 0               a code, by elim_W at the large motive Ty 0
coh(sup(a; f)) ⇝ Π(y : Below(a)). Σ(_ : Id(A; root(f y); fst y)). coh(f y)
```

`coh` is where the synthesized ih first appears: the recursive occurrence
`coh(f y)` in the body *is* `ih y`, the λ-bound ih applied at an abstract
position. Then

```text
Acc(a) := Σ(t : W₀). Σ(_ : Id(A; root t; a)). El (coh t)
```

Read: a tree, a proof its root is `a`, and a proof that every subtree's root
is the position it hangs at (hereditarily).

`coh` is a *code* rather than a type because `elim_W`'s motive must be a
`Type` and we are eliminating into `Ty 0` — large elimination, exactly as
`branching_route_a.rs` does for its position family. `El` decodes it
(`reduce.rs`, El of every extension code), so `El (coh (sup(a; f)))`
weak-head-reduces to the `Π`/`Σ` above with no explicit coercion.

## 2. `intro` and the eliminator

### 2.1 `intro`

Given `a : A` and `h : Π(b : A). Π(_ : R(b, a)). Acc(b)`, write
`ch y := h (fst y) (snd y) : Acc(fst y)` and

```text
f      := λ(y : Below(a)). fst (ch y)
intro(a; h) := ⟨ sup(a; f) , refl(A; a) , λ(y : Below(a)). ⟨ fst (snd (ch y)) , snd (snd (ch y)) ⟩ ⟩
```

Three things have to hold *definitionally* for this to check, and all three
are the round's subject matter:

1. `root (sup(a; f)) ⇝ a`, so `refl` discharges the index component;
2. `El (coh (sup(a; f)))` reduces to the `Π`/`Σ` shape — the ih fires under
   the λ at an abstract `y`;
3. `root (f y) ⇝ root (fst (ch y))`, so the certificate `ch` already carries
   at `fst y` is exactly the one the coherence component wants. **No
   transport, no funext.**

### 2.2 Well-founded recursion

Given a motive `P : A → Ty 0` and

```text
st : Π(a : A). (Π(b : A). Π(_ : R(b, a)). P b) → P a
```

produce `wfrec : Π(a : A). Acc(a) → P a`. The tree is the recursion
variable; the index proof and the coherence proof ride along in the motive:

```text
M(z) := Π(a : A). Π(_ : Id(A; root z; a)). Π(_ : El (coh z)). P a
```

Step, with `a₀ : A`, `f : Π(y : Below(a₀)). W₀`, `ih : Π(y : Below(a₀)). M(f y)`:

```text
λ(a : A). λ(q : Id(A; a₀; a)). λ(c : El (coh (sup(a₀; f)))).
    J(q) ▸ st a₀ (λ(b : A). λ(r : R(b, a₀)).
                    ih ⟨b, r⟩ b (fst (c ⟨b, r⟩)) (snd (c ⟨b, r⟩)))
```

`c ⟨b, r⟩ : Σ(_ : Id(A; root (f ⟨b,r⟩); fst ⟨b,r⟩)). El (coh (f ⟨b,r⟩))`, and
`fst ⟨b, r⟩ ⇝ b`, so its first component is precisely the index proof
`ih ⟨b,r⟩` needs at `b`. `J` transports `P a₀` to `P a` along `q`.

```text
wfrec(a; acc) := elim_W(…; M; step; fst acc) a (fst (snd acc)) (snd (snd acc))
```

### 2.3 The equation this round exists to observe

With `a`, `h`, `st` all **open variables**:

```text
wfrec(a; intro(a; h))  ≡  st a (λ(b : A). λ(r : R(b, a)). wfrec(b; h b r))
```

definitionally — no `Id`, no transport in the statement. Both sides reduce
to the same term: on the left `J` fires because the index proof is `refl`,
the synthesized ih `λy. elim_W(…; f y)` β-reduces at `y := ⟨b, r⟩` to
`elim_W(…; fst (h b r))`, and the coherence λ β-reduces so that
`fst (c ⟨b,r⟩) ⇝ fst (snd (h b r))`. That is the recursor's specification,
stated at the openness the frontend would need.

## 3. The instance: the ω+1 order

The scheme is generic in `(A, R)`; the test instantiates it at the smallest
relation that is genuinely **infinitely branching**, which is what puts `Acc`
in tier 2 in the first place (no uniform arity ⇒ `Wf` cannot hold it):

```text
A := Unit^0 + Nat        ω := inl ⟨⟩        num n := inr n
R(b, a) := El (case a of inl _ ⇒ (case b of inl _ ⇒ Empty | inr _ ⇒ Unit)
                       | inr _ ⇒ Empty)
```

Every numeral sits below `ω`; nothing sits below a numeral; `ω` does not sit
below itself. Well-founded, one node of infinite arity, and no arithmetic —
`R` is two nested `case`s into `Ty 0`.

Accessibility, both through `intro` alone:

```text
h_num n := λb. λr. elim_Empty(r)              since R(b, num n) ⇝ Empty
acc_num n := intro(num n; h_num n)            n open

h_ω := λb. case b of inl _ ⇒ λr. elim_Empty(r) | inr n ⇒ λr. acc_num n
acc_ω := intro(ω; h_ω)                        infinite branching
```

(The `case` generalizes `r` into its motive — `Π(_ : R(b, ω)). Acc(b)`,
which mentions only `b` — so each arm sees the reduced `R`. The same
generalization `branching_route_a.rs`'s `mirror` does for its ih.)

A computing recursion at the constant motive `P a := Nat`:

```text
st := λa. λk. case a of inl _ ⇒ Succ (k (num 5) ⟨⟩) | inr _ ⇒ Zero
wfrec(ω; acc_ω) ⇝ 1
```

which is a value produced *through* an infinitely-branching node: the step
picks one position out of `Nat`-many and the ih is applied there.

## 4. What this predicts about funext, and about `Empty`-η

The standing claim ([[kernel-v1-extensions]], `docs/branching-inductives-
choice.md` §1) is that tier 2 buys funext as an axiom. **This scheme
predicts it does not — not for the recursion direction.** Funext is needed
to prove two `W` *trees* equal (route A's leaf arm,
`crates/elab/tests/branching_route_a.rs`), and nothing above states an
equation between trees: `Acc`'s components are consumed, never compared.
The prediction to check is that `crates/kernel/tests/acc.rs` postulates
nothing in Ψ.

Same for [[empty-eta-conversion]], which names "tier-2 `Acc` under `W`" as
its concrete customer. `elim_Empty` at an absurd position discharges the
leaf arms *computationally* here, because they are children functions
being **built**, not compared. If the file is green with an empty Ψ, that
customer is not this construction, and the issue should say so.

Where funext would still be required, and is out of scope: proving `Acc(a)`
is a proposition (any two accessibility proofs equal), which is what you
need to show `wfrec` is independent of the accessibility proof supplied.
Not needed for defining or computing with `wfrec`.

## 5. Acceptance checklist

1. `W₀`, `root`, `coh`, `Acc(a)` are well-formed, `a` **open**.
2. `intro(a; h)` checks with `a`, `h` open — §2.1's three definitional facts.
3. `acc_num n` checks with `n` **open**; `acc_ω` checks (infinite arity).
4. `wfrec` checks at an **open** motive and step (`p`, `st` context
   variables), not only at the closed `Nat` instance.
5. **The β equation, §2.3, with `a`, `h`, `st` open.** Headline.
6. `wfrec(ω; acc_ω) ≡ 1` — closed computation through the infinite node.
7. Negative: coherence is load-bearing. `intro(ω; λb. λr. acc_num 7)` —
   children about the wrong index — is **rejected** by `check_term`.
   Without this the fording could be vacuous.
8. Negative: `wfrec(ω; acc_ω)` is not convertible to `0`.
9. Negative: at a **neutral** scrutinee (`acc` a variable) `wfrec(a; acc)`
   does not fire — not convertible to the §2.3 right-hand side.
10. Ψ is empty (§4).

## 6. As landed (2026-07-26)

`crates/kernel/tests/acc.rs`, 13 tests, all ten checklist items met.
**Ψ is empty in every one of them**, so §4's prediction holds: the recursion
direction of tier 2 buys no funext, and `Empty`-η is not its customer.
The scheme needed no change; four things are worth recording.

1. **`ih` takes the position first.** `elim_W` hands the step
   `ih : Π(y : Below a₀). M[f y/z]`, not `M`-with-extra-arguments, so
   `wfrec`'s step applies it *four* times: `ih ⟨b, r⟩` to pick the child,
   then the index, the index proof and the coherence proof that `M`
   abstracts. Writing it as three applications type-checks nowhere near the
   mistake and reports `NotConvertible` at the outermost `check_term` — the
   one place in this construction where a bug hides, and worth stating
   because a tier-2 generator has to emit exactly this spine.

2. **`Unit`-η is load-bearing in the `case` arms**, exactly as
   `docs/inductive-tier0-lowering.md` §3.3 needs it for the nominal tag. An
   arm of `case b of inl u ⇒ …` must be given at `R(inl u, ω)`, and the arm
   binds `u : Unit`; the arm is written at `R(ω, ω)` and conversion bridges
   it because `u ≡ ⟨⟩`, hence `inl u ≡ ω`. Without `Unit`-η the arms would
   need their own transport. `Empty`-η, the rule
   [[empty-eta-conversion]] expected to want here, is not involved: the leaf
   arms *build* children with `elim_Empty` rather than comparing them.

3. **The coherence predicate is load-bearing, and is checked to be.**
   `intro(ω; λb. λr. acc_num 7)` — children about the wrong index — is
   rejected twice over (the field at `h_ty`, and the `intro` at `Acc(ω)`).
   Without that control the whole fording could have been vacuous.

4. **Cost.** The file is the slowest in the workspace (~25 s of the suite's
   ~40 s), all of it in the two conversions that unfold `coh` under a λ.
   Nothing pathological — `coh` is a code-valued `elim_W` and every
   comparison re-reduces it — but a tier-2 *generator* emitting these terms
   at volume should expect conversion, not elaboration, to be its bottleneck.
