Found by the 2026-09-13 checkpoint job (is the perf detour over?). The ignored rail `generated_20_node_mixed_binary_alu_dag_checks` (crates/elab/tests/function_ir_x86_scaling.rs) fails at 20, 50 and 100 nodes in ~7 s: `the kernel rejected mixed_binary_alu_x86_artifact: OutOfFuel`. It passed at 30.9 s on 2026-09-06 ([[function-ir-x86-proof-scaling]] log). The five-node default in the suite still passes, which is why nothing caught it.

**Bisected** (git bisect run, each step a furnace job, dev profile, 8 MiB test stack, `NATIVE_DEPTH_LIMIT` at its committed 1024): good at bc07438, **first bad 9ae0855** ([[per-instantiation-type-codes]], 2026-09-10). Cleared on the way: the ofNat respelling (rail still fails with lib/std/bitvec/bitvec.hthr at c62a3d3's text), the depth budget alone (94b7694, which carries it, passes in 56 s), the whole K1 line and the deferred kernel gate (813b2b9 already fails).

**Both budgets are exceeded by one query.** With `NATIVE_DEPTH_LIMIT` 4096 the run lasts 19 s instead of 7 before the same refusal; with fuel 2^26 and depth 16384 (256 MiB stack) it passes in 22 s. Under those budgets the artifact item is 3.94 s of a 5.54 s module kernel gate, 1,058,158 evaluator steps, 5.06 M drives, 228 misses; the conv-miss table charges it to four `Id`/`Id` misses (3.78 s of the row's 4.01 s). At five nodes the same item is 0.028 s / 11,864 steps — 89× the steps for 3× the instructions, so the cost is superlinear in the block.

**Mechanism (hypothesis, the shape §13.10 of docs/inductives/per-instantiation-type-codes-design.md already measured on `caller_native_memory_same_28`, 8–64× DEFAULT_FUEL):** the artifact's fields are stated as `after_name(before).rip = final_rip(before.rip)` etc.; the receiver bottoms out in the local `before`, so the per-instantiation projection reference for `MachineState<M>` is minted, and a projection reference is cheap to compare and dear to compute through. Computing through it here means the symbolic execution of the whole 22-instruction block over an open state, once per field. The four misses should have been syntactic shortcut hits (the generated statements are the record's field types after substitution): whether the miss is a spelling twin (the record's substituted field type vs the theorem's stated type spelled through different references) or a genuine compute-through is the first thing to attribute — a decline trace in project_field by receiver shape, §13.1's method, and the two sides of the first Id/Id miss.

Not a tuning question: raising fuel or depth makes the rail pass and hides a 140× item. The bar is the artifact item back near its five-node cost per node, the rail green at 20 and 50 under the committed budgets, and the rail promoted into a gate that runs (it is `#[ignore]`d, which is how a week passed).

Repro: `HEATHER_FUNCTION_IR_X86_NODES=20 HEATHER_TIMINGS=- cargo test -p heather-elab --test function_ir_x86_scaling generated_20_node_mixed_binary_alu_dag_checks -- --ignored --nocapture --exact` (ulimit-wrapped; probes/bisect_remote.sh is the furnace step).

## Log

- 2026-09-13: Attributed, fixed and the rail promoted. **The twin, not a compute-through**: the item's four `Id`/`Id` misses are `BlockArtifact`'s four `Id`-typed field types, whose `after.x` reads carry the `MachineState#projⱼ` **reference** (elaborated at the declaration, where `after` is a bare binder), met by the generated theorems' **inline walk** (their receiver `…_after(before)` is global-headed, which §13.6 declines). The decline listing is unambiguous: five `MachineState` reads with `gate=false args=true recv=App<App<App<Global`, i.e. `rec_proj_ref_at` would have fired and only the receiver-shape gate refused. So §13.6's boundary was **not stable under substitution**, and the shortcut is all-or-nothing per type, so the whole `Id` fell to evaluation — the 22-instruction symbolic execution, once per field, superlinear in the block. Attribution, both sides and every rejected candidate: docs/archive/x86-scaling-rail-out-of-fuel-design.md. **Fix**: `receiver_admits_reference` — neutral **and not a bare `Var`** (§2.5). Two alternatives were hand-run on the current tree first and both re-broke `caller_native_memory_same_27/28`: minting at every receiver (so §13.10's dated rejection has *not* expired) and bounding the computation at one layer (reused_callee's own receivers are one global over three neutrals — no receiver-shape predicate separates the workloads). **Result**: 20-node rail OutOfFuel -> ok 4.6 s warm / 16.7 s cold, 50-node ok 8.9 s, both under the committed budgets. The artifact item is now *independent of the block*: 4,263 evaluator steps and 279 misses at **both** 5 and 20 nodes (7.32 / 7.57 ms kernel), against 1,058,158 steps and 3.94 s before under lifted budgets — the bar asked for 20-node steps within 5x the five-node count and they are equal. **Cost**, both arms one box one build: reused_callee item total 88.06 -> 90.99 s (+3.3 %), kernel_gate 60.98 -> 62.77 s (+2.9 %), steps 46,617,036 -> 46,744,997, misses 361,641 -> 381,625 (+5.5 %, and named: bare-local reads back on the walk, ~90 µs each). The ±3 % bar is met on kernel_gate and missed by 0.3 points on the item total. **Promoted**: the 20-node case is now `slow_acceptance_generated_20_node_mixed_binary_alu_dag_checks` — no `#[ignore]`, run by the full lane, filtered by the fast lane's own prefix — and it is a refusal-shaped gate needing no metrics; 50 nodes stays an explicit `#[ignore]`d stress rail.
