An investigation, asked for by Minus: could the new reflected languages of `docs/language/ownership-design.md` (level 1, level 2, and the steps of a place) be typed by construction, and what stands in the way?

[[ownership-steps]] chose untyped steps interpreted in one universal value type, for two reasons it recorded: a step typed by its source and target is an inductive family indexed by `Type`, "a universe above what the kernel evaluates cheaply (Tarski universes; no data quantifies over `Type` in the library)", needing an existential over each intermediate type; and a shape universe with a list of values inside the value type is a nested inductive, which the elaborator refuses (`docs/inductives/mutual-inductive-design.md` §1), so it wrote a mutual group. Every reflected language in the tree is untyped syntax with typing recovered by synthesis, presumably for the same reasons. Neither cost has been measured.

## Scope

A measurement and a judgment, with small probes; no change to the kernel or the elaborator.

- **A family indexed by types.** Write typed steps as a family over `Type` (source and target), a chain of them, their denotation, and an apartness decider on two chains. Measure what the kernel pays to elaborate the declarations and to evaluate the decider and a denotation, against `lib/lang/ownership/steps.hthr` on the same inputs. Say where the cost is (the recursor's motive, the gate, conversion at the indices, universe levels) and whether it is a shape or a constant.
- **A family indexed by codes.** The same with a small data universe of shapes as the index and a decoding to types. Measure the same things. Say what the elaborator refuses on the way (a nested occurrence, a decoding in an index) and which existing design note owns each refusal.
- **What exists.** `issues/indexed-families.md`, `issues/indexed-families-at-any-index-type.md` (closed), `issues/indexed-family-recursion-gate-cost.md` (closed), `issues/nested-inductives-container-fields.md` (open), `docs/inductives/` (`inductive-indexed-lowering.md`, `per-instantiation-type-codes-design.md`, `wf-shape-code-design.md`, `mutual-inductive-design.md`), `crates/kernel/DESIGN.md` on universes and codes. Read them first; much of the answer may be written down.
- **The judgment.** For each of the three languages the design needs (steps; level 1's terms; level 2's values and operations): would typing by construction remove real proof work (name the lemmas in `steps.hthr`, `value_steps.hthr` and `place_check.hthr` that exist only because a term may not fit), what would it cost, and is the blocker the kernel, the elaborator, or neither.

## Done when

The probes are in `lib/_tests` (or a scratch module named in the log if they should not stay), the measurements are reported as shapes with what dominates, and the log gives a recommendation: stay untyped, index by codes, or index by types, with what would have to be built for the second or third.

## Log

- 2026-11-25: Measured, on branch `claude/typed-reflected-languages`. Probes kept as pins: `lib/_tests/lang/ownership/typed_steps_tests.hthr` (both families, what works and four refusals under `expect-error`). Measurement modules were generated in a scratch directory and not committed (`gen.py`/`gen2.py`: three modules per length, untyped `steps.hthr`, type-indexed, code-indexed, chains of element steps at lengths 4 to 64, each a closed decider verdict, a closed read through the denotation, and the denotation converted to the library's composite path at an open value, all by `refl`). Release binary, `--kernel-workers 1`, fresh `--cache-dir` per run, read with `scripts/timings`.

  **The answer in one line**: the kernel's evaluation is not the cost and not the blocker. Indexing by types is blocked by the type theory (no type former is provably injective or disjoint), not by the kernel's speed; indexing by codes works and costs a constant factor per code node, which is the kernel's data encoding, times a depth-quadratic size that any typed chain carries. Neither removes proof work on balance for steps. Stay untyped.

  **1. Indexed by types.** `inductive S : (s: Type) → Type` is refused by the elaborator ("an index is a `Nat` or a previously declared inductive", route 1's check in `Inductives::index_type`); the `IWf` route's open index (a type parameter instantiated at `Pair<Type, Type>`) would need the result indices computed from value parameters, which are not in scope there ([[value-params-in-result-index]]); but a declared index type whose constructor holds two types is accepted: `inductive TyIx { Ix(s: Type, t: Type), NoIx }` and `TStep : (st: TyIx) → Type` with `TElem(t: Type, i) : TStep(Ix(List<t>, t))`, `TFst`, `TSnd`. The chain `TSteps` (the existential over each intermediate type is a field `m: Type`), the denotation `TSteps::path : Path<TyIx::src(st), TyIx::tgt(st)>`, and the decider all check, and the decider evaluates by `refl`; induction over the chain at open data works. A type parameter instantiated at `Type` (`Fam<Type>`), a record with a `Type` field and `List<Type>` all elaborate; "no data quantifies over `Type`" was a habit, not a refusal. **What fails is every proof that compares two steps out of one source.** After matching the first step at a variable index, the second is at `Ix(List<x>, B)`, which the elaborator does not invert, and a convoy owes `Pair<a, b> = List<x>` refuted (refused: "`absurd` needs an equation at a declared inductive type") and `List<x> = List<y>` to give `x = y`, which nothing in the theory proves: there is no typecase on `Type`, so type formers are neither injective nor disjoint, propositionally. So the decider's soundness cannot be stated usefully, whatever the kernel's speed. A step's target could be fixed by its source only through that injectivity. Cost, for completeness (all small): the declarations a few ms each; the decider and the read evaluate in time linear in the chain and no dearer than the untyped ones; building a chain or a value is cheap in the kernel (a type compares in about one query per node) but superlinear in the elaborator on nested type applications ([[nested-type-application-elaboration-cost]]), and a chain of depth `n` writes types of total size quadratic in `n`.

  **2. Indexed by codes.** `Shape` (word, sequence, vector of a length, record, variant, store) with `Shapes` as a mutual group (`List<Shape>` inside is nested and refused, owned by [[nested-inductives-container-fields]]; a container field `[Shape; n]` is accepted but its decoding needs a type passed as a value to `fold`, which the surface refuses), the decoding `Shape::el` by mutual large elimination (one function per group member, so a record's product and a variant's sum are one `Shapes::el(fs, sum: Bool)`: two functions on `Shapes` are refused, `mutual-inductive-design.md` §6), and `CStep : (st: CIx) → Type` over `CIx { Cx(s, t: Shape), NoCx }`. A typed field position as a family over `Shapes` (`Mem`) is refused: a group member is not an index type, and the message is generic ([[mutual-member-as-index-type]]); fields and variants use a `Nat` position and a computed target `Shapes::at(fs, i)` instead. Everything then checks: the five kinds denote as the library's paths at the decoded types (the window is `Path::window` into `[A; n]`, lawful everywhere: no `fits`), the decider evaluates by `refl`, induction over chains works, and inversion of a step at a source code is a convoy closed by `absurd` on codes (`seq_step_kind`). The full soundness proof was not written; its one missing piece is UIP at the code type ([[uip-from-decidable-equality]], Hedberg, a cheap lemma): the second step's convoy equation `SSeq(y) = SSeq(x)` gives `y = x` by injectivity, and the goal's transport along it is `refl` only by UIP. A `match` at a constructor-headed index whose constructor has payload arguments still has to write every impossible arm: no inversion at an index constructor with payload arguments (recorded for forded families in `docs/archive/inductive-fording-lowering.md` §14, item 8).

  Shapes, from the doubling series 8 to 64: evaluation (the verdict, the read) is linear for all three (the untyped verdict's steps were quadratic only because of its `[…]` literal: [[list-literal-evaluation-quadratic]]). Building a typed chain is quadratic in its depth: each step names its intermediate type or code, so the index a chain carries has total size about `n²/2`; for codes the kernel's queries grow fourfold per doubling (a code literal costs about 25 queries per node to check, against about 2 per node for a type: codes are inductive values, checked and compared through the `Wf` encoding node by node), for types the kernel stays linear and the elaborator's time on nested type applications is what grows. Decoding a value's type (`sh(n).el()`) is the same shape. At the depths places have (under ten steps) this is a constant factor: codes about five to ten times the untyped cost to check a place, types about one to two. The declarations themselves cost a few ms each in all three; nothing was seen in universe levels, the recursor's motive or the family's signature. So, to Minus's question: the per-node constant is our kernel's way of holding data (the code encoding), the quadratic is the existential (inherent to typed chains without sharing), and the blocker for types is the theory, not the kernel.

  **3. The judgment, per language.**
  - *Steps.* What exists only because a step may not fit: `path.hthr`'s section "Laws where what is written fits" (`PathLawsOn`, `Path::KeepsFit`, `PathLaws::on`, `Path::then_laws_on`, `Path::then_keeps_fit`, `Apart::under_on` and its parts); `steps.hthr`'s `StepModelLaws.keeps`, `Steps::keeps_fit`, `Steps::verdict_keeps`; and about half of `value_steps.hthr`: the carriers (`Vals`, `VSlots`, `list_of`/`of_list`), the steps into the universe with their `set_none` arms (`seq_laws`, `rec_laws`, `map_laws`), the window's `fits_seq`, `vec_put`, `vec_path`, `vec_laws_on`, `fits_parts`, `vec_read_fits`, and `sel_keeps_len`, `keeps`. Codes remove all of it. They add: UIP at codes, injectivity of the code constructors, a convoy per same-kind pair in the one-step soundness, a heterogeneous `same` soundness (one spelling at one source has one target), and apartness of two field or variant positions in nested pairs by induction (the untyped model gets that from `PathIn::apart_sound` on lists). Variants stay partial either way. And the decider reads atoms symbolically: a typed window's target `SVec(n, a)` needs `n` as a value, so typed steps live after the atoms are read (the `Sel` level) or the family is indexed by the words too. Roughly a wash in proof size, a constant factor worse in checking, and the untyped one is built and proved. Blocker for types: the theory. For codes: none, one missing lemma.
  - *Level 1's terms* (a function's body in A-normal form; `place_check.hthr` is its first fragment). What exists because a term may not fit: `list_at`, `buf_at`, `view_at` with their `unwrap_or` defaults, "a binder must be the next name of its space" in `check`, `Atom::bound`, `Atom::val_stable` with `Prefix`, `holders_upto`'s range, `Inv`'s lengths. Well-scoped syntax (a family over `Nat` counts, route 1, the cheap index) would remove the defaults and the bound checks; `val_stable` becomes weakening, not nothing. Well-typed syntax (contexts as lists of codes) is the typed-syntax family whose recursion cost was superlinear in constructors ([[indexed-family-recursion-gate-cost]], since fixed: flat per-`refl` cost at twelve and thirty-two constructors), and every reflected node would carry its context: the existential above at program scale. `rt.hthr`'s `Tm::sem` already shows the alternative that works: typing by synthesis, casts that vanish at closed codes. Stay untyped; scope by a checker pass.
  - *Level 2's values and operations.* Values typed by codes are `Shape::el` (no `Val` at all), but a heap of blocks of different shapes needs a heap typing, and a `Ref`'s type depends on it: every store must keep the heap typing, which is `KeepsFit` again in another place. By types it is impossible for the reason in 1 (a heap of `Σ T: Type. T` cannot be compared). The obligation moves, it does not go. Stay untyped: `Val` is already the code universe's untyped twin.

  **4. Recommendation: stay untyped, for all three, and do not hold up §16's three interfaces.** Indexing by types is out (the theory). Indexing by codes is possible and could follow later without rework: a code-typed universe is one more `StepModel` (its value type `Σ c: Shape. c.el()`), so the interfaces as built (untyped steps denoted through a model) do not preclude it. What it would take, if a later customer wants typed values: [[uip-from-decidable-equality]] (small), the soundness convoys and position apartness (a module the size of the fit machinery it replaces), optionally [[mutual-member-as-index-type]] for typed field positions; and a constant factor in checking places that no kernel work on conversion alone removes, since each code node is data.
