The present derivation has traversal bounds, but a useful cost story also needs proof size, conversion work, and certificate reuse. This is separate from the representation cost of large dependent dictionaries.

## Acceptance

- Measure visited nodes, reduction steps, certificate probes, emitted proof size, and kernel checking on composed and shared subexpressions.
- Show how costs scale with nesting, repeated uses, branches, and opaque certificate boundaries. Include refusal at exhausted budgets without claiming the theorem false.
- If memoization is introduced, key it by the actual environment, operation, relations, assumptions, and binder scope; test invalidation and proof-relevant evidence distinctions.
- Preserve sharing where useful and recheck reused artifacts. Separate cold/warm search costs from kernel conversion costs.

Customers: named augment and conditional augment on both backends. Related: [[relational-certificate-registration]], [[setoid-dictionary-projection-growth]], [[proof-benchmark]], [[setoid-auto-lifting]].

## Scheduling

Levers, cheapest first: cache the projected (field, Π type) per (dictionary, index) for the derivation's lifetime (removes ≈6.5 s of 12.7); only search families when the node's operation head is itself a field projection of a dictionary in scope, or test the field's codomain head against the goal's before the kernel check (removes most of the remaining ≈5.5 s); the repeated searches over the same op at successive expansion depths are the memoization case the acceptance bullet describes. The synchronous kernel checks are now inside the kernel_gate timer and the expansion reducts inside elab_whnf, so a `--timings` row shows this split without ad-hoc probes.
- 2026-09-12: The first two levers of the entry above, landed in `Builder` (`crates/elab/src/relational_context/proof.rs`). (1) The projected `(field, Π type)` of every dictionary reachable by name from `cx.dicts` is computed once per derivation instead of once per search: only the innermost binder of a name is ever searched and the walk pushes nothing but freshly named aliases, so the `Σ`-spine walk has the same answer every time. (2) Before the candidate `field(fixed)` is kernel-checked at the goal, the candidate's own type — the field's codomain at `fixed` — is converted to the goal in the check's own world and at the check's fuel (`core_converts`, charged to `kernel_gate` like the check). That is a necessary condition of the check asked without re-checking the projection chain, so nothing the kernel would accept is lost, and the laws fields that take an operation and then say something else about it (`member_image`, `image_union`, `image_empty`, `image_composition`) fail there for a fraction of a failed conversion's cost.
