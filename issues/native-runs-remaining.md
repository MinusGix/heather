The command's remaining hand-threaded phases and the other process libraries,
moved onto `Runs` (`docs/native-runs-design.md`, `lib/elf64_runs.hthr`).

- **The command** (`lib/elf64_command.hthr`): done. The last chains (the
  total row, the prologue, the spill's row setup) moved 2026-10-17; the
  spill's slots stay on the `Runs` rules, where they cost less than half
  of a stretch (`docs/native-straight-runs-design.md`, "Not yet"), and
  `closes_runs` on them around its system call. `inputs_runs` only
  composes other phases.
- **The process libraries**: onto the frame-free stretch
  (`lib/elf64_stretch.hthr`). The argument scan is done (its letter table
  and refusal stay by hand); the stat pass and the operand cursor are
  done, and so are the row writer and the read filter's chunk loop (a
  program of its own, the step its hole).
- **The list layouts** left in `elf64_read_pieces`, `elf64_read_filter`
  (the program around the chunk loop, and the loop's close JZ and write
  loop after it) and `elf64_count_print` (`layout_code`/`layout_at`):
  parked as [[list-layouts-onto-labels]]; their logic is Heather's already.

Acceptance: each moved phase checks in no more kernel steps cold than
before; `build-x86` of wc writes the same bytes.

## What it assumes from elsewhere

- **[[native-straight-code-triples]]** (closed) owns the rules. Assumed
  here: only `lib/elf64_runs.hthr` and the command's frame rules as landed.
- **[[native-symbolic-execution-step]]** owns generating these chains.
  Assumed here: nothing; this work is by hand and is what that step would
  emit.

## Log

- 2026-10-10: **Five phases moved onto `cmd_straight` instead** ([[native-symbolic-execution-step]], `docs/native-straight-runs-design.md`): `loop_last` (absorbing `cursor_store_runs`, `last_tested_runs`, `last_test_block`), `operand_head`, `stdin_path`, `open_back` and `failed_runs`. Their `Runs` chains are now one call each, which states no middle predicate. `closes_runs` stays on the rules: a system call ends a stretch, and its JCCs sit on both sides of the call. The phases this issue lists as not yet moved are unchanged.
- 2026-10-15: **The dash test and `open_path` moved onto `cmd_straight`**, with a `LoadByte(ByteLoad)` item in `x86_64_asm` that `Straight` reads (`ByteThen`: the caller names the byte and its address, read under every frame). The dash test is two stretches (`dash_test`, three items; `dash_ahead`, four) with the name's case analysis in small lemmas (`first_byte`, `stdin_of_*`): cold, its kernel steps fell to well under half the hand-threaded proof's. A first draft, one seven-item stretch per case of the name, took more steps than the hand-threaded proof; `docs/native-straight-runs-design.md` ("Where a stretch forks on data, split it"). `StdinAt`/`OpenAt` take a framed state, so neither closes over the starting frame. wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-15: **The name's scan and write, the read's failure and the loop's cursor moved onto `cmd_straight`**, once their continuations took a frame: `name_scan` (one five-item stretch per byte, over `NameReads`/`NameFound`), `name_send` (two stretches, ending in a `FramedWrite`), `read_failed` and `loop_cursor` (continuation-passing, ending in a `CursorGoes`). Cold, each phase's kernel steps fell, by about half (`loop_cursor`) to four fifths (`name_scan`, `read_failed`); the elaborator's weak-head time on each rose from milliseconds to seconds, the trade the earlier moves made ([[elab-whnf-copies-straight-continuations]]). `init_runs` as one twenty-one-item stretch took more than twice its chain's steps and was left as it was. wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-16: **The row's tail and `init_runs` moved onto `cmd_straight`.** `x86_64_asm` has a `StoreByte(ByteStore)` item, and `Straight` carries the page as it carries `vars` (every continuation and leaf takes the page first; a byte store hands on `page_stored`, the caller naming the offset in `PageThen`). Stretches written once over any label carry most of it: `init_runs` is the width's three-item stretch then nine calls of `zero_runs` (a slot zeroed, ending at `zero_label(before, q)`); the tail's two arms are the count's test (`tail_test`, a continuation per side of the JE) and a byte sent after the row (`byte_sends`, ending at `byte_label(before, name, w)`, then `row_sent`); `newline_send` is one three-item stretch. Cold kernel steps: the tail (with the old `row_send`) fell to about a third, `init_runs` (with the old `zero_slot`) to about a seventh; elaboration rose from about half a second to about two for the tail and by about the one check of `zero_runs` for `init_runs`. Written out per phase instead, the tail took over half again the steps and about four times the elaboration. The other `Straight` phases' steps did not move. The command's `nonzero: 0 < n` is `n: Pos<Nat>`. wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-17: **The total row, the prologue and the spill's row setup moved onto `cmd_straight`.** `total_runs` (four items to the JB) and `total_rows` (the setup's five items, the row, " total\n" as seven calls of `put_runs`, a `StoreByte` stretch of three items written once over any label, and R9/R10 before the write); `keep_runs` (two stretches), `keep_single`, `begin_runs` and `begin_test`; `spill_row`'s setup after the spill's chain. Cold, the elf64_command module's kernel steps fell by about a fifth; the total row's phase to under a third, the prologue's and `begin`'s to about a third, the spill's row setup to about half. Two `omega`s for the count's bounds had been three quarters of `keep_runs`'s steps (an `omega` reads every hypothesis in scope); the phases' arithmetic is in small lemmas now. The spill's slots as a stretch (`state_gpr(spill(i))` is not a constant, so its reads are stuck in the computed type) took over twice the chain's steps and were left on the rules. wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-18: **The libraries' phase `omega`s moved into lemmas**, before any library moves onto stretches. An `omega` inside a hand-threaded phase pulls the phase's `let`-named states into its certificate through any register fact sharing an atom with the goal; one in `stat_operand` was two thirds of its kernel steps. Cold, over `elf64_command`'s whole closure the kernel's steps fell by about three tenths: the stat pass, the cursor, the filter and the row writer to about half, the formatter by about a quarter; no item rose but the new lemmas. The stat pass is still the dearest library (`stat_operand`, `acct_stated`), and the next step: a stretch that runs outside the command's frame. wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-18: **A frame-free stretch for the libraries** (`lib/elf64_stretch.hthr`, `docs/native-straight-runs-design.md`, "The libraries' stretches"): `Straight` without a frame, the shared pieces moved there from `elf64_straight`/`elf64_command`. The stat pass's `acct_stated` and `stat_operand` moved onto it (its loads are now `Load`/`LoadByte` items, the same bytes). Cold, `acct_stated`'s kernel steps fell to a fiftieth, `stat_operand`'s (with `stat_each`) to a twentieth, the stat pass's to a fifth and the command closure's by a quarter; `stat_end`, not moved, rose a few percent. Left in the stat pass: `stat_end`, `stat_done` (hand back an end state; moving them means a continuation through `StatOut`). wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-19: **The argument scan moved onto the frame-free stretch** (`docs/native-straight-runs-design.md`, "The libraries' stretches"): the setup, each argument's head to the dash test (`scan_arg`, absorbing `scan_head`, `option_first` and `scan_option`), the dash's tail (`after_dash`, absorbing `after_dashes`), the letters around the table, the operand, the null slot and the default's test; the phases' ends restated over `LinuxSteps`. Cold, the scan's kernel steps fell to two fifths (`after_dash` with `after_dashes` and `scan_runs` to under a quarter, `letters_run` to about a quarter, the argument's head to about half) and the command closure's by about a twelfth; no item rose by more than a few dozen steps. The one-step loads and `run_store` moved into `elf64_runs`. wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-19: **The stat pass's end and the operand cursor moved onto the frame-free stretch** (`docs/native-straight-runs-design.md`, "The libraries' stretches"): `stat_end_runs` (the cursor, then four items) and `stat_done` (the total's digits, then five items), both continuation-passing, so `stat_end` is gone; the cursor's argument (`cursor_arg`, eight items, absorbing `cursor_head`), its dash (`cursor_dash`, twelve items, absorbing `cursor_dashes` and `cursor_option`), the null slot and the start, with `CursorReach` and the start's end over `LinuxSteps`. Cold, the stat pass's end fell to a little over a quarter of its kernel steps, the cursor's to about two fifths (the start to under a third) and the command closure's by about a twelfth; no item rose but the new wrappers. wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-19: **The row writer moved onto the frame-free stretch** (`docs/native-straight-runs-design.md`, "The libraries' stretches"): a stretch per path through a slot (not selected, the first field, any other field with its space), the formatter by hand between, and `skip` to the back jump; continuation-passing through `SkipThen` and `RowReach`. `row_loop` itself fell to a sixtieth of its kernel steps, the row writer's to about three fifths, and the command closure's by about a fortieth. wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-20: **The producer's layout table is gone; the filter's and the command's jumps and code come from the library** (`docs/native-architecture-review.md`, after "Decision 2"). Each jump is a Heather function of where the library's layout places it (`core_jumps`, `filter_jumps`, `cmd_jumps`, `last_jumps`), landing by library theorems proved by evaluation (`core_jumps_land`, `filter_jumps_land`, `wl_jumps_land`); the producer evaluates the layout over the generated pieces' bytes and spells the code in literal chunks. `crates/cli/src/native/layout.rs` and Rust's read head, failure tail and write loop are deleted; `asm_same`, the landing lemmas and `chunks_same` moved to `x86_64_layout`. Cold, the command closure's kernel steps rose by about a thousandth, all of it the new landing lemmas. The read filter's byte loop on stretches, and the list layouts on labels, are still left. wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-20: **The read filter's chunk loop moved onto the frame-free stretch** (`docs/native-straight-runs-design.md`, "The libraries' stretches"): the loop is an `x86_64_asm` program (setup, load, the step as its `Hole`, store, advance, JNZ, close) placed first in the core's layout, so `FilterJumps` loses the JNZ's displacement, which lands by `loop_again`, proved by evaluation at the step's length. A byte is a one-item stretch to the step, the step by hand, and a three-item stretch to the JNZ whose sides hand on through `ByteAgain`/`ByteDone`; the setup and the close are one item each, the chunk's end stated over `LinuxSteps` (`ClosedReach`). `filter_entered`, `filter_again`, `filter_done` and `filter_stored` are gone. Cold, a byte's path fell to about a sixth of its kernel steps, `filter_loop` by about a third, the filter library's to about three fifths and the command closure's by about a thirtieth; no item rose by more than a few hundred steps. wc's bytes are unchanged.
- 2026-10-20: **Closed; the list layouts are parked as [[list-layouts-onto-labels]].** Their jumps and positions are Heather functions since the jump-table round, so the move is uniformity only, over a small slice of the command closure's cold kernel steps, and a rewrite of the write loop's, read head's and failure tail's proofs. Every phase this issue named is on `Runs` or a stretch.
