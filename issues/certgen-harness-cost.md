Accumulator issue (perf convention: observations filed small and triggered,
one deliberate round per session). Two measured numbers from the v2 round
(2026-08-02), both fine today, with levers recorded for when they are not:

- **The two double-j kit lemmas** (\`letcall_conv\`/\`letmatch_conv\`) cost
  ~9s release EACH to elaborate — the step_mono term-size price (two
  interpreters in scope under a rewritten scrutinee), paid once per
  elaborator that loads them: once in \`lib/ir_v1.hthr\`'s full check
  (51s -> 70s release) and once per certgen-harness elaborator.
  Levers: an inversion record a la BindInv for the k_letcall/k_letmatch
  fragments (BindInv retired three convoy motives at -12.6%); elaborator-
  computed generalized motives ([[ih-generalization]]).
- **The certgen harness** is ~108 CPU-s in one elaborator: ~50s support
  prelude (step_mono + the kit re-elaborated), ~50s the five emissions.
  Scales linearly-ish with emission count, so a v3 that generates dozens of
  functions blows past the per-process rail. Levers: an elaborator
  snapshot/clone so the prelude is paid once per suite rather than per
  test binary; trimming step_mono's cost (above); checking emissions via
  the release CLI against a concatenated file instead of debug-profile
  in-process elaboration.

Trigger: certgen harness > ~200 CPU-s, or a third consumer of the kit
lemmas' elaboration cost appears.

- 2026-08-12: The 163x rule of thumb above is NOT retired, but it is restated and its wording was wrong (deliberate perf round, [[wf-field-conversion-cost]], which now owns the mechanism and the numbers). Wf-lowering is not the operative property: Refs and Val are Wf-lowered too and their lowered forms are 58 and 80 nodes, i.e. free. What costs is the SIZE of the field type's lowered form, which is quadratic in its constructor count -- Ir is 1712 nodes because it has eight constructors -- and the elaborator's tier-1 match lowering writes the container's own lowered type out ~21 times per arm, each copy carrying the field types verbatim. So: an inductive field whose type has many constructors multiplies every match on the container by Theta(arms x |lowered field type|), and the kernel then does work strictly linear in the term it is handed (no conversion pathology; peak RSS flat). Read the rule as "carry what you need of a large type, not it", and read the 163 itself as unreproducible: the faithful reconstruction of the design doc's recorded draft shape is 8.5x, ~30 CPU-s with the six Cert eliminations the doc also mentions. The fix is named and sized on that issue and belongs to the [[family-types-as-code-defs]] family, not here.
- 2026-08-12: **Rails re-measured after [[wf-field-conversion-cost]]'s fix round, and the "don't embed" rule of thumb is NARROWED — not retired.** Release-CLI numbers on the concatenated-file harness the perf rounds use (`lib/ir_v1.hthr` cut at its first `theorem` + the file under test), best of three, A/B on one binary via a registration kill-switch: the declarations prefix alone 0.88 → **0.62 s**; prefix + `lib/certgen_cert.hthr` 1.67 → **1.15 s**; prefix + `lib/certgen_code.hthr` — the shells file, this suite's second-most-expensive binary — 34.80 → **30.65 s**. Elaborated body nodes on that last one 19.5 M → 16.2 M. **The rule of thumb still applies**, because only half the mechanism was fixed: naming `⟦D⟧` once removes the copies of the *whole* lowered type, and leaves the copies of its *components* (the spine's Σ payload types and sum tails, the application `dom`/`cod` annotations, the projection chains). So "carry what you need of a large type, not it" stands; what changes is the constant, by roughly 2x on a container-with-large-field workload, and the residual now has a measured attribution on that issue rather than three unweighed suspects. **The hypothesis this issue's rails motivated is refuted**: the 52-branch `match code` shells are not that pathology (`check_sig`'s body is 15 k nodes with *zero* `Type::Wf`; `transcribe`'s is 620 k, 3.3% of the file) — what dominates `lib/certgen_code.hthr` is its fifteen ordinary program-layer `match`es on `TA`, whose `TBody(body: Ir)` field is the 1712-node type. **No fast-lane lever to record**: the improvement is a broad constant-factor across every elaboration rather than a floor drop at one test, so `scripts/test`'s skip list is unaffected and untouched.

That +6 s per elaboration was enough to push crates/elab/tests/certgen.rs's TWO tests past the 300-second per-process ulimit -t (SIGKILL, and the awk summary reported a truncated 129-passed total rather than a failure -- the same mis-read shape this issue already records, seen again). Generating a non-Nat fixture is ~3 s (cheaper than a Nat one at ~10 s -- these bodies are two instructions), so the round's cost is almost entirely the shared library elaboration, which every certgen binary pays once.
