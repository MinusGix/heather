Stage 2 of `docs/language/ownership-design.md` §17.5, second part: the bytes level (`lib/native/bytes_level/`, [[bytes-level-language]]) lowered to x86 under the contract stated once (`Site::Meets`, `Injected`; [[x86-memory-contract]]), with its theorem proved once for every program, beside FunctionIR's lowering and not through it.

## Scope

- **The register invariant at the lowered values.** A word id holds its word as today (`File::Represents`, `Held`); a reference id holds its target's address under the injection (`Value::lower`, as the experiment found: the kind lives in the value, not the register file). Validity's kinds (`Program::valid`) are what make a wrapped node's FunctionIR equation hold of the lowered values: no word node reads a reference.
- **Leaves.** A run of `Compute` nodes is FunctionIR's leaf, lowered by `lower_nodes` and proved by `lower_nodes_keeps`/`lower_nodes_guards` unedited, at `NodesHold` over no buffers (a pure node's evaluated step satisfies its equation: `Node::holds_of`). Each memory node lowers to one instruction through the reference's address: a load or a store of 1, 2, 4 or 8 bytes (`MOV`/`MOVZX`), a reference's load and store (`MOV`, as a word's), `Offset` as an `ADD`/`LEA`, and `Copy` as a byte loop or `REP MOVSB`. Each is proved from bytes_memory's site facts (`Memory::take_some`, `put_some` and their corollaries) and one `Injected` lemma for `take` and one for `put`, since every operation is one of the two.
- **Control and calls.** Branches and loops reuse `StructuredRegion`'s emission (the label scheme, `File::meet`, `LoopRegisters`, `loop_entry`, `loop_exit`) and cite the theorems the reading found to be about words and control (`emits_seq`, `branch_emits`, `loop_rounds`, `epilogue_moves` and the rest in [[bytes-level-language]]'s log). A call is an ordinary call on values under `Site::Meets`. A window is two parameters, so nothing renumbers memory.
- **One decision first, the coordinator's.** The reading found that the call sequence, the loop's passes and the function's frame in `function_ir_lower_calls` reach memory only through `Stack::Trail`, and only for its stability under the frame's own stores. Either `EmitsOver` and its four companions (`Stack::ArgsReady`, `Called`, `Entered`, `Closed`) and `Site::loop_passes` are made generic in a state type and a memory clause with that one law (then this lowering restates none of them), or this lowering restates them over `WroteIn ∧ Injected`. The log of [[bytes-level-language]] states the generalisation exactly.
- **The heap is the contract's** (since [[bytes-level-on-the-contract]]): `Heap` in `x86_64_memory_contract`, every operation a read or a write of a `Piece` (`Heap::piece`, `Heap::placed`) with one site fact (`Heap::fits`) and partial forms `take`/`put`; `Blocks` (which blocks a run writes) is the language's, passed to a put as `written`, and `Heap::put_lent` turns a put the run makes into a store a lending allows where `Blocks::Lent(spans)` holds. The injection lemmas are per machine store (`Loan::store_injected`, `store_ref_injected`, `Injected::reads` with its widths over `read_bytes`, `ref_read` over `load_ref`); the general one for a put or take of any piece, which the typed copy needs, is [[contract-put-of-a-piece-injected]]. A word load's meaning is `little_endian` of the bytes read (FunctionIR's `buffer_word`), the machine's `immediate_from_bytes` of them: the leaf lemma relating the two is this issue's.

- **`Site::Meets` as built** (`x86_64_call_contract`, with the meaning's `Value`, `Answer`, `Ran`, `Calls()`; a module of its own because FunctionIR's lowering imports the memory contract and spells its own `Word` and `Answer` bare). The machine with the world is `Running { state, io }`; a step (`Site::Step`) is a machine step with the world unchanged, or a system call the Linux model reads off the registers (`Site::Asks`: `LinuxSyscallAt`, `LinuxRequests`, and the world's answer one `LinuxCmd::Answers` allows), the process resumed as `linux_answered` allows and the exchange recorded. `Site::Performs(goal, calls)` is the claim form (steps to `goal`, or to a call and, from every resumption `LinuxResumes` allows, performs it again). For routine `f` entered as `Site::Entering` says (at its label, arguments passed as `lower_values`, the heap injected, the world `Io::Answerable`), at every fuel: *done* (`Site::MeetsDone`): `run` done with `a` means the code performs `Site::Returned` (the return address, the stack pointer above it, `a`'s words lowered in RAX and `answer_registers()`, `callee_kept()` kept, `WroteIn` the lending, `a.heap` injected, the world `a.io`); *exited* (`Site::MeetsExited`): `run` exited with `status` and `o` means the code performs the exit call with `status` in world `o`; *going* (`Site::MeetsGoing`): every `Running` the code reaches by `Site::Step`s before it returns (`Site::Before`) has a world whose history is a prefix of one `run` reaches. The record `Returned` is gone: what a callee answers is `Answer`, its words lowered. The probe `calls_lent_meets_done` (`lib/_tests/std/arch/x86_64/x86_64_call_contract_tests.hthr`) gives the done clause from `Site::CallsLent` at an empty shadow. What `Meets` at the entry routine buys a whole program is [[call-contract-whole-program]].

## Done when

`lower_correct` for programs: whatever the placement, a program the lowering returns, resident anywhere, meets `Site::Meets` for each routine at every fuel (each `Done`, `Exited` and history of `run` realised as above). The bytes level's probes compile, each with its theorem, among them the deleted experiment's lowering probes (a typed copy of a record holding a reference lowers to the same `MOV`s as a word copy; a placement putting two values in one register is refused).
