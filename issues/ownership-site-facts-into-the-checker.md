`lib/lang/ownership/site_facts.hthr` added, beside `call_check.hthr` and without touching it, what a source proof needed to reach a residual ([[ownership-residuals-from-source-proofs]]). Each belongs in the checker when it is next reshaped, not in a second module over it.

## Scope

- **Residuals as propositions over the final state.** `Fact::Holds(pres, s: Sem)`, not `Owed::Holds(pres, words)`: a bound reads a version's length, which no word holds. Each open residual stated as the proposition the source writes (a bound as a squashed `<`), so the source's proof is the discharge by conversion. Apartness: a residual naming its side (the window before the element, or after) instead of the Boolean verdict over the disjunction, which today needs `omega` because the verdict spells `0 + k <= j` against the source's `k <= j`.
- **Bounds emitted by the checker** (`bounds`, one per element read), and through a view (`Read`), which `site_facts` does not bound.
- **Preconditions over the parameters' values** (`FnSafeAt`): `pick(xs, i, inside: ∥i < xs.len()∥)`, the arena's `read`, has a precondition that reads its list.
- **`Pre` at the lent window's value**: `Pre(g, args)` holds `pres(g, vals(args, words))`, over the call's words only, so a callee whose precondition reads its parameter (the arena's `read` called from a caller) cannot be called. And a call of a reader that answers a word (no `CallMut` or `CallView` shape is one).
- **The branch over any bound word**: `Branch` tests only the words given, because its run and its meaning must agree on the test and `FnSafe` does not export `Sim.words`. A test over a word the body bound is the checker's own branch.

## What it assumes from elsewhere

- **[[ownership-checker-loans-and-joins]]** owns the checker's branch and join. Assumed here: residuals under a branch carry its test as a hypothesis the discharge can name (`decide_true` of the convoy's equation), as `branch_sound` does.
- **[[ownership-residual-discharge-restated]]** owns the form the discharge is asked in.

## Done when

`check_sound` itself states residuals over the final state in the source's vocabulary, with preconditions over parameters and `Pre` at the lent window, and `site_facts_tests` passes against it without `site_facts`.

  **What changed, one form of each thing.**
  - *The words* (`Env`): parameters read by position, the body's words newest first with their count. Replaces the snoc list (`Sem.words`, `Exec.words`); `Prefix`, `Atom::at`/`at_stable`, `Env::get_push_below`/`get_push_at`, `Env::start_get` replace the list lemmas. See [[ownership-meaning-word-spine]].
  - *Residuals* (`Owed`): `Apart(lo1, n1, lo2, n2)`, the disjunction `end1 <= lo2 ∨ end2 <= lo1` with `Atom::end` spelling a window's end as a source does (`n` from zero, `lo + n` elsewhere, so an element `j` after a window is `j < lo` by `Lt`'s definition); `Inside(sj, i)`, `∥i < sj.val(s).len()∥`, one per `Get` and per `Read`; `Pre(g, sj, args)`, `pres(g, sj.val(s), sj.lent(s), vals(args))`, at the value the call lends (`Subject = OfPlace | OfView(t) | OfLent(ts)`). `Due(Obligation)` and `Under` are gone (the Boolean `Spans` verdict decides closed atoms only, `apart_owed`). The checker emits `Apart` only where every atom is bound, so a residual read at its site reads alike in the later words the invariant uses (`apart_sound`).
  - *The verdict* (`Owes = OEnd | ODo(here, rest) | OIf(a, b, rest)`, `check_fn: Option<Owes>`): what a function owes is `Owes::Holds(d, w, pres, body, s, after)`, each statement's residuals at the state it reads (`Owed::Then`, so a statement that owes nothing adds nothing), a branch as `Bool::Cases(test, arm a then what follows, arm b then what follows)`; `Owes::Due` from the first state. **Not over the final state, as this issue's scope said**: with the write parameter a place (the fourth experiment), the final state holds no earlier value of it, and arms bind different numbers of sites, so a log indexed statically fails at a join. Reading each residual at its site is the faithful form: it is the source's context at that site.
  - *The meaning* (`mean_then<R>(w, ops, s, k)`): a branch is `convoy(test, arm then k, arm then k)`, the convoy a source's `if h:` elaborates to, carrying what follows into each arm; `mean_body` is it at the identity and `mean_lent` at `mean_out`, so a tail `if` pins by `refl` (`poke_reflects`, `hop_le_reflects`). `mean_then_is` relates the two (stated with `R` a value argument: polymorphic recursion is not lowered).
  - *Contracts*: `pre: List<Nat> → List<Slot> → List<Nat> → Type` (object value, lent slots, words), `post: … → MOut → Type` (the whole answer); `pres`/`posts` alike; `Posted` and `fn_safe_answers` likewise. Owing every bound forced both: a read through a callee's view needs a postcondition on the view (`returned`, `beside`), a lending callee's read through what it was lent a precondition over the slots (`fill2`), a read after `CallAll` a postcondition on the owner's length (`pair`).
  - *`CallGet(g, args, z)`*: a reader of the whole object answering a word, licensed as a read of the whole object (`check_get`, `Sim::asked`, `Sim::got_core`, `step_get_call`). A reader can also be lent a write view's window with `CallMut` (`peek_at`).
  - *`check_sound`* asks the residuals and the postcondition at the parameter words spelled (`spell`, `words_eta` moved in), so a generator states a function's discharge at the source's locals, one lemma for every function; `Bool::cases` is the shape of an arm's proof.
