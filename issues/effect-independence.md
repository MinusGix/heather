A reordering pass that consumes `Writes` facts (`docs/compilation-vision.md`
§3.2) needs to know that operations with disjoint footprints commute and
that a reordered sequence means the same. `docs/surveys/native-mathematics-survey.md`
§8; survey rank 10.

## Scope

- An independence relation on actions (disjoint footprints); trace
  equivalence as the congruence generated by swapping adjacent independent
  actions (Mazurkiewicz traces).
- Equivalent traces have the same effect on a store; on worlds, the same
  answers at a world separable at the actions' resources.

## Done when

A pass swapping two independent statements cites the theorem rather than
proving the swap.

## What it assumes from elsewhere

- **[[world-separable-frame]]** owns separable worlds. Assumed here: only
  its separability predicate.
- **[[address-interval-sets]]** owns memory footprints.
